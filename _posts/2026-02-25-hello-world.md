Welcome to the new VICIP group blog! This post explains **why we set it up** and **how you write your own post**.

## Why a group blog?

- New papers deserve more than a single tweet that vanishes in a day.
- Methods notes (statistics, code, fieldwork tricks) are useful to others in the group — and to the internet.
- Writing short summaries of your own papers is great practice and makes your work more discoverable.

## How to publish a post

1. Create a file in `_posts/` named `YYYY-MM-DD-your-title.md`.
2. Start it with a small header block (copy the template in `archetypes/post.md`).
3. Write in Markdown, then push to `main`. GitHub rebuilds the site automatically in ~1 minute.

```markdown
---
layout: post
title: "My post title"
date: 2026-03-01
author: Your Name
tags: [statistics, ed]
excerpt: "One or two sentences shown on the homepage."
---

Your text here. **Markdown** fully supported, including code blocks,
> blockquotes, and lists.
```

## What's next

Expect posts on upcoming projects, conference trips and the occasional methods deep-dive. If you're a group member and want to contribute, just ask for repo access — or open a pull request.
